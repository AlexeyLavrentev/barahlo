# Phase 13 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> Sources: `13-CONTEXT.md` (D-01..D-06 — ALL locked, used verbatim; discretion areas resolved in Defaults), `13-RESEARCH.md` (Patterns 2–4, Pitfalls 1/4/7, island shape, zone placement recommendation, plural recipe), `REQUIREMENTS.md` (DEL-01, DEL-02), `ROADMAP.md` §Phase 13 SC1–5, approved `12-UI-SPEC.md` (the byte-exact register this phase extends: destructive family, «Не удалять»/«Удаляем…», DELETE_ERROR), implemented code (`app/(app)/(card)/devices/[id]/page.tsx` — card layout, the `status !== 'disposed'` conditional at :246, PhotoGrid as the last section child, `Value` mono convention), `app/(app)/(card)/devices/[id]/movement-edit-dialogs.tsx` (MovementDeleteConfirmDialog — the dialog being generalized, `HINT_CLASS`/`ERROR_CLASS`), `app/(app)/employees/archive-confirm-dialog.tsx` (standalone wrapper-island shape), `app/(app)/devices/device-actions.tsx` (the WRONG home for the zone — bypassed for disposed), `lib/ru.ts` (`Intl.PluralRules('ru')` form-table recipe), `app/globals.css` (tokens; `--color-hairline: #D2D2D7`), `components.json` (`registries: {}`).

**Phase character:** ZERO new design decisions at the system level — tokens, typography, spacing, accent discipline are frozen. The phase adds exactly ONE new surface to the card (a quiet destructive zone visible for ALL statuses, disposed included) and ONE dialog that is the third installation of the red-confirm recipe. The destructive family gains its third member: solid `#D70015` fill moves from «Списать» + «Удалить запись» to also cover «Удалить» device. Success has NO visual state on the card — the action redirects to `/devices` (D-05); the only fresh mechanics are the identity block (model + serial/inventory) and the plural-aware counters line.

## Spacing Scale

Inherited from 02-UI-SPEC unchanged: xs 4 / sm 8 / md 16 / lg 24 / xl 32 / 2xl 48 / 3xl 64. Standing exceptions hold (44px min interactive height `min-h-11` via extended hit-area for quiet text buttons; 12px half-step `mt-3`/`gap-3` allowed; `mt-1.5`/`py-2.5`/`px-2.5` forbidden).

Phase 13 specifics:
- **Delete zone (the one new card region):** the section's LAST child, AFTER the `PhotoGrid`, rendered OUTSIDE the `device.status !== 'disposed'` conditional (`page.tsx:246`) — never inside `device-actions.tsx` (that whole component returns null for disposed; Pitfall 4). Geometry: `mt-8 border-t border-hairline pt-4` — the `mt-8` cadence matches every FieldGroup, the hairline separates the danger zone from the photos, Apple "danger zone at card bottom" position. Single left-aligned trigger inside.
- **Zone trigger:** quiet text button, 14/400 destructive red at rest; the 44px touch rule met by an extended hit-area (`after:`-inset / negative-margin pattern — phase-12 row-trigger precedent), not visible padding.
- **Dialog shell:** `max-w-md p-6 *:min-w-0` — byte-parity with MovementDeleteConfirmDialog. Identity block rows stack `space-y-1` (4px); counters hint sits `mt-3` below the identity block; error paragraph (`role="alert"`) directly above the footer form (archive-confirm-dialog order); footer = `DialogFooter` with the two buttons `gap-2` — no scroll cases (no fields; standing `max-h-[85svh] overflow-y-auto` rule not needed).

Exceptions: none new.

## Typography

Inherited unchanged — exactly 4 sizes, exactly 2 weights (400 + 600; no 500 anywhere):

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tracking | Tailwind |
|------|------|--------|-------------|----------|----------|
| Label / secondary | 14px | 400 | 1.45 | 0 | `text-sm` |
| Body | 16px | 400 | 1.5 | 0 | `text-base` |
| Heading | 20px | 600 | 1.2 | -0.01em | `text-xl tracking-tight` |
| Display | 28px | 600 | 1.2 | -0.02em | `text-[28px] tracking-[-0.02em]` |

Role assignments for this phase:
- **Heading (20/600):** dialog title «Удалить устройство?» via the existing `DialogTitle` recipe. No page headings change.
- **14/600 (the allowed third weight application):** the model line in the dialog identity block — mirrors the phase-12 delete dialog's record-label role («Выдача»…): the "what exactly is being destroyed" headline.
- **Label (14/400):** zone trigger, identity labels («Серийный номер», «Инвентарный номер»), counters hint, error paragraph, pending/dismiss buttons.
- **Mono 14/400 (`font-mono text-sm`):** serial/inventory VALUES in the identity block — the card `Value` convention; «—» when null (no mono on the dash).
- **No new sizes, no new weights.** Body 16 appears nowhere in this phase's UI (the dialog is content-only, like «Удалить запись?»).

| Destructive | `#D70015` | (a) **NEW: zone trigger text at rest** (`text-destructive`) — the quiet red that scopes the danger zone; (b) **NEW: solid fill of the device-delete confirm button** (white 14/600, hover `bg-destructive/90`) — the family's third member; (c) error paragraph `ERROR_CLASS` = `text-sm text-[#D70015]` (byte-exact family); (d) «Списано» pill tint and the two existing red fills (dispose primary, timeline-record-delete primary) — unchanged |

**Destructive discipline evolution (phase 12's one semantic decision, now at device scope):** red fill stays reserved for genuinely irreversible destructions — «Списать», «Удалить запись», and now «Удалить» device. Content inside the dialog stays NEUTRAL ink — the red marks the *consequence button* and the *quiet zone scoping it*, never the content (model/serial/counters render in ink/ink-secondary). The quiet zone trigger is red AT REST (unlike the neutral timeline row triggers) because it is a standalone danger zone at the card bottom, not an action inside a record — the color is the scoping device that makes a single quiet text button legible as "this deletes the whole device".

Accent reserved for (the standing list, NO additions):
1. Primary CTAs and focus rings (no page-level CTA appears in this phase).
2. Combobox affordances (no picker appears in this phase).
3. Dialog primary submits of the create/edit/transition family — **explicitly NOT the device-delete confirm** (destructive, not accent).

### What does NOT change

- The custody actions row, its status matrix, «Редактировать»/DeviceDialog, «Клонировать», photos (upload/delete/lightbox), timeline rows and their «Исправить»/«Удалить» triggers, FieldGroups — untouched.
